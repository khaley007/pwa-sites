How do hour caps, time tracking and billing work?

Our assistants clock in and out, and hourly clients pay only for the hours actually worked. There is no hourly minimum, so you can begin with 1 to 2 hours a week.

You can set an hours or budget cap. We notify you as the cap approaches, and you decide whether to approve extra hours. That keeps a busy week, such as Easter or Christmas planning, from turning into a surprise invoice.

You also choose how your assistant works. A strategic assistant takes a goal, proposes a plan and waits for your approval. A hands-on assistant follows the process you already have. We match the type to what you want.

Our services are backed by a 14-Day Satisfaction Guarantee. If you are not fully satisfied within 14 days, we will work with you to make it right or offer a hassle-free resolution.

Is it safe to give a virtual assistant access to church systems?

Yes, when the rules are clear and you keep control of the logins. Here is how we handle it:

  • NDA. Every Pure Water Automations assistant signs a non-disclosure agreement.
  • Two-factor authentication on your phone. For shared logins, we recommend that you tie two-factor authentication to your own phone and re-authenticate your assistant every 1 to 2 weeks.
  • Vetting through people we know. Most of our assistants are referred by someone who knows them personally.

A practical rule on your side: give access only to the tools and folders the work needs, and review that list each time you re-authenticate.
