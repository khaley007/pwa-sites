Our Heart

Serving the Providence, One Task at a Time

Pure Water Automations was founded in June 2025 by Justin Okamoto and Eunmi Rangala-Maddox, two members who watched leaders they love carry heavy administrative loads on top of their providential responsibilities, and decided to do something practical about it.

Our name comes from True Mother's words: pure water is clear, life-giving, and continually moving in order to stay pure. We aim to be that stream for the leaders we serve; refreshing, faithful, and always moving.

Aligned with the Vision of Dr. Hak Ja Han

We believe every hour freed from paperwork is an hour returned to Heavenly Parent's work: to witnessing, to raising families, to caring for the people entrusted to each leader.

Our Mission and Vision

Mission

Pure Water Automations helps mission-driven leaders remove administrative burden through practical systems, automation, and operational support.

Vision

To become the most trusted operations partner for ministries and mission-driven organizations, combining trained human assistants, documented workflows, and practical AI.

What We Hold On To

Excellence

Collaboration
